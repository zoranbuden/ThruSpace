ThruSpace — Technical Abstract
ThruSpace is an AI‑native spatial geometry engine designed for deterministic interval‑based reasoning, modal consistency, and declarative spatial layout. The system integrates three modal frameworks — CART, POLAR, and SPHERE — enabling stable geometric behavior across Cartesian, radial, and spherical domains. A unified Interval Geometry Model provides continuous spatial transitions, contact‑point semantics, and constraint‑driven structural validation.

ThruSpace introduces a declarative layout language (TSL) that expresses spatial relationships through high‑level rules rather than imperative positioning. This allows XR/AI systems to infer structure, resolve constraints, and maintain modal coherence automatically. The engine supports multi‑modal transformations, global spatial inference, and deterministic contact logic, making it suitable for XR environments, robotics navigation, AI scene understanding, and geometry‑driven simulation.

By combining interval geometry, modal reasoning, and declarative layout, ThruSpace establishes a consistent computational foundation for next‑generation spatial computing systems.
